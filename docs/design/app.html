The refreshed layout

Everything the app has today, arranged on purpose instead of by accretion — a navigator rail that never disappears (so collapse is reversible by itself, which is the only reason the header existed), one update surface, a status line that elides, and a dock with a vertical tab rail and a scope. Six screens. The toggle swaps the entire design language: every colour, face, radius and weight is a token, so nothing but the language changes. See _layout.md.

What the refresh fixes

  • The rail never disappears. Collapse is reversible by the rail itself, which removes the reason the header existed as a workaround. Compare the Session and 2×2 screens — the list panel is gone in one, and nothing is stranded.
  • One update surface. ↓ 0.21.0 lives in the command bar whatever else is open, instead of a card when the sidebar shows and a banner when it does not.
  • The status line elides. Model and permission mode always — the two that change behaviour — and the rest behind ⋯. Round two's One Bar proved nine facts do not fit; this shows two and admits the rest.
  • Dock tabs go vertical and gain a scope. Tabs stop eating the narrowest panel's width, and this pane / all answers who a tab belongs to — the question Catch could not resolve.
  • The pane header stays on top. One Bar moved it to the floor and the render showed the name wrapping to three lines.

What is still open

  • Signal's tints need a decision. At 11–14% they sort a column but do not shout. Stronger reads as decoration; weaker is invisible. The delegated-agent violet is the one that clearly earns itself.
  • Sheet has no way to float. The palette here uses a border and a shadow, which is the one place the language breaks its own no-elevation rule. It may simply be the honest exception.
  • Light mode is not an inversion for Signal. A 12% wash over a dark ground disappears over a light one; it needs its own scale.
  • The rail's icons are placeholders. Six glyphs doing six jobs needs real drawing, and the badge convention is asserted rather than designed.
  • Motion is undesigned — streaming, the progress hairline, focus transitions, and what the palette does on open.
