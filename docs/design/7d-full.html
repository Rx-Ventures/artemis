7D · CONSOLE — THE FULL PASS

Console, everywhere

The chosen treatment on the whole application. 8px radii, alpha hairlines at 7% (12% on inputs), sans chrome in sentence case, surfaces as computed lifts, a composer that reads as a card — applied not to one conversation but to the nine surfaces the conversion actually has to pay for.

The arrangement here is Artemis's own — session list, pane grid, dock — not round seven's simplified three-region shape. Round six's arrangement question stays open; this answers what the app looks like, on what ships today.
