Five marks, five idles

Both drawn in the Sheet language and its verified palette. The mark today is a bow — Artemis the huntress — and it is also the app icon, so whatever replaces it has to be redrawn in build/icon.svg too. Every candidate below stays inside the Artemis reading; none of the idles touch the bow, as asked.

Each mark is shown at 96px, 32px, 20px and 16px, and reversed on an accent fill. 16px is the real test: the current bow's string is 15/512 of the box, so it thins below a pixel under ~20px and the mark collapses to an arc.

02Antler

The stag — Artemis's animal, and the one thing in her iconography that is hers alone. A bow belongs to any archer; the deer belongs to this goddess. Six strokes at one weight, symmetric, square caps. Nothing in lucide is close to it, so it needs no geometry spent on avoiding a neighbour.

Risk: the most decorative of the five, and the one most likely to read as a hunting-lodge brand rather than a developer tool. It also has the most strokes, so it is the first to muddy at 16px — check that size carefully.

The hunt without the weapon: a trajectory and the thing it arrives at. One rule on the diagonal, three faint stations along it, a filled square at the end. Sheet's own grammar as a mark: a square machine frame around a round human moon, split on the terminator line. 05Monogram

Just the A, constructed rather than typeset — three strokes at one weight, square caps, apex left sharp. It survives anything: 16px, a favicon, a monochrome print, a menu-bar template icon. And it is the only one that cannot be mistaken for another app's glyph, because it is a letter.

Risk: it says nothing. No hunt, no moon, no idea — it is a wordmark's first letter, which is the safe answer and the forgettable one. Worth considering only if the identity is going to live in the name rather than the mark.

What picking one commits to

  • The mark is the app icon. components/logo.tsx and apps/desktop/build/icon.svg are the same four paths on purpose, and the .png/.icns are rasterised from the svg — so a change is three files and a re-export, not one component.
  • 16px is the gate. Antler is the one at risk; Monogram and Waypoint are safe by construction; Aperture and Terminator hold because their thinnest element is a 24/512 stroke rather than the bow's 15/512 string.
  • Idle 03 is not independent. It is mark 04 in motion. Any other pairing needs the idle chosen on its own merits.
  • None of this is applied. Say which mark and which idle and I will draw them properly — icon svg, png, icns, the component, and the reduced-motion path — before touching anything else.

If you want a recommendation

Antler, with Scan. Antler is the only mark here that is Artemis's and nobody else's — a bow is any archer's, a moon is every theme toggle's, an A is any product's — and it has no neighbour in the icon set the app already ships. Its one real risk is 16px, which is a drawing problem I can solve with a reduced two-tine variant rather than a reason to reject it. Scan pairs with it because it shares nothing with the mark, so the two never compete, and it is the only idle that stays obvious at pane width in a 2×2.
